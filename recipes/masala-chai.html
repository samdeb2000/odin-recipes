Masala Chai

Description

Masala chai is a popular beverage originating from India. It is made by adding aromatic herbs and spices to chai, which is made from brewing black tea (usually crush, tear, curl) in milk and water, and sweetening with sugar, often using aeration techniques only performed by masters or "tevanas".

Overview

  • ⏲️ Prep time: 5 min
  • 🍳 Cook time: 10 min
  • 🍽️ Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 8 g fresh ginger
  • 10 cardamom seeds
  • 1.5 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoon black tea

Steps

  1. Peel off the ginger and cut it to small pieces.
  2. Crush ginger and cardamom seeds.
  3. Bring 1 l water to boiling.
  4. Turn off the stove.
  5. Add ginger, cardamom seeds, black tea and milk to the water and cover the pot.
  6. Wait 5-10 minutes for infusion.
  7. Serve with a teaspoon sugar per cup. You may want to sieve, or wait for the particles to precipitate to the bottom of the cups.

Recipe based on Indian tea by Pestanzùl.
